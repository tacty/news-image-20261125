A one-image front page summing up the world's news of Wednesday, November 25, 2026,
in the winner-template broadsheet design: a cream broadsheet with a big date
masthead, 29 dated stories across 7 regions, a UTC timeline, "The Day in Numbers",
a dotted world map with pins, and a DATE CHECK footer. Two oil tankers are hit by
mines and left ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz; the US proposes tariffs on 14 trade
partners; Ukraine says it damaged five jets at Russia's Savasleyka air base; China
launches its first foreign astronaut aboard Shenzhou-24; and Roma end PSG's perfect
Champions League run with a 2-2 draw in Paris. Every headline was verified against
two sources. The program writes the bundled image to /output/entry.png — no network,
byte-identical on every run.
